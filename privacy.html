eDompet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Applies to the eDompet app for Android and the eDompet app for HUAWEI watches.

eDompet is a wallet for your membership and loyalty cards. It is built so that your cards stay with you: the app has no account, no server and no internet access.

Data the app stores

The cards you add: the store name, the card number or QR/barcode content, the code type, the card color you choose, and whether a card is pinned. This data is stored only on your phone, and on your HUAWEI watch if you use the watch app.

Data we collect

None. eDompet does not request the Android internet permission, has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and does not send your data to the developer or to any third party.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap Scan code to read a card's QR code or barcode. When you choose Choose photo, the Android photo picker gives the app only the picture you select; it is read on your phone to find the code and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Your HUAWEI watch

If you connect a HUAWEI watch, eDompet sends your cards from your phone to the eDompet app on your watch over the Bluetooth connection managed by the HUAWEI Health app (HUAWEI Wear Engine). To do this, eDompet reads the name and connection status of your paired watch and whether the watch app is installed. The cards are stored only on the watch. eDompet asks for your permission in HUAWEI Health before it connects, and you can withdraw it in HUAWEI Health at any time. HUAWEI Health is provided by Huawei and has its own privacy policy.

Backups

Android may include the app's data in your device's own backup (for example, your Google account backup), if backup is turned on in your phone's settings. This backup is managed by Android and your account provider, not by eDompet.

Deleting your data

Delete a card in the app, and it is removed from your phone and from your watch. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your phone; uninstalling the watch app removes the cards from your watch.

Children

eDompet is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
